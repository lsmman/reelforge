Concept art of a colossal derelict humanoid mech kneeling among the ruins of a flooded city, overgrown with vegetation. Painterly digital matte painting, industrial design realism.
Scale reference: a single human figure standing on rubble in the lower left, tiny against the machine.
Lighting: flat diffuse light from a heavy overcast sky, aerial perspective washing out contrast with distance so the far towers read as pale silhouettes.
Detail density high on the mech's shoulder and chest armor plating with visible panel lines, weld seams, rust streaks and creeping ivy; loose and atmospheric everywhere else.
Palette: cold slate #13181C, weathered steel #2D3436, moss grey #6C736E, pale fog #A3ACAD, misted white #D9E1DF.
Composition: 21:9 ultra-wide, mech filling the right two-thirds, ruined skyline receding left.

Avoid: flat lighting, stock photo look, unreadable silhouette, symmetrical mush, cartoon proportions, text overlay.
