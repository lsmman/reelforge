Concept art of a sunken ancient temple colonnade on the seabed, shafts of light cutting down between the pillars. Painterly digital matte painting.
Scale reference: a single diver silhouette drifting near the base of one column, small against the architecture.
Lighting: god rays refracted through the surface far above, split into hard shafts between the columns, suspended particulate catching the light, deep falloff into blue-black at the edges.
Detail density high on the nearest carved column capitals and the coral encrusting them, loose and hazy in the depth behind.
Palette: bright cyan #46BECA, teal #26859C, ocean blue #1B607A, slate blue #1E3442, deep #0D2C39, near-black #042536.
Composition: 16:9, symmetrical colonnade receding to a lit vanishing point, foreground column cropped at the left edge.

Avoid: flat lighting, stock photo look, unreadable silhouette, symmetrical mush, oversaturation, text overlay.
